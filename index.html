Good questions, clear answers

Before you choose a plan.

Building is a big undertaking. Here are a few details to help you start with confidence.

Ask us a question →
Are these plans ready to build?

Plans include construction drawings as described on each plan's product page. Local codes, site conditions, engineering, zoning, and permitting requirements vary; have your local building professional review and adapt the documents as needed.

What is included in the PDF or CAD package?

The PDF package provides a digital construction set. PDF + CAD adds editable CAD files intended for use by qualified design and building professionals. Exact file contents vary by plan and are listed with its package options.

Can I make changes or reverse a plan?

Modification consultations and right-reading reverse options may be available for an additional cost. Use the modification request form to describe what you have in mind; availability and pricing depend on the plan and requested changes.

Can I build the home more than once?

A standard purchase includes a single-home construction license. An additional build license may be purchased where offered. Plans and CAD files are copyrighted and may not be copied, resold, or redistributed.

Are plans engineered or guaranteed to meet my local code?

Do not assume engineering or local code compliance is included. Requirements vary by jurisdiction, site, and project. Your local building department and qualified professionals can advise what review, engineering, or adaptation is required.

How will I receive my plan, and are purchases refundable?

Digital formats are intended for delivery after purchase; printed sets, where offered, are shipped. Delivery timing, file access, and purchase-change or refund terms should be confirmed in the plan's checkout and license terms before placing an order.

Can you design a completely custom home?

Fieldhouse is a collection of residential plans with modification conversations available. A fully custom design engagement is not implied; contact the team to ask about current services.

Before you build

House plans are design documents intended as a starting point for your project. Building codes, engineering, site conditions, zoning, and permitting requirements vary by location. Plans may need review and adaptation by qualified local professionals. A plan purchase includes a limited license for construction as described in the package details; plans and CAD files remain protected by copyright and may not be redistributed. Additional build licenses are available for purchase. Engineering and code compliance are not represented as included unless specifically stated for a plan.
