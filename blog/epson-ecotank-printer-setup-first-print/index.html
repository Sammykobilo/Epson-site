Epson EcoTank Setup Guide: First Print and Common Checks

April 14, 2022

Follow a careful Epson EcoTank setup checklist for unpacking, ink preparation, initial charging, paper loading, and print-quality verification.

Follow the setup guide for your exact EcoTank model

Epson EcoTank printers share a refillable-tank design, but setup details differ by model. Remove shipping materials as instructed, place the unit level, and fill tanks only using the bottles and process specified in the model manual. Do not operate the printer before completing any required ink charging step.

Load paper and complete the first print checks

Load supported paper with the guides aligned and complete the initial printer setup prompts. If the first page has missing lines or unusual color, print a nozzle check and follow the manual rather than repeatedly running cleaning cycles.

Keep the printer ready for routine use

Use the printer periodically according to the manual, store paper in a dry place, and check ink levels through the printer’s recommended method. When an alert appears, capture the exact wording and model before searching for an Epson EcoTank troubleshooting or service solution.

Can I skip the Epson EcoTank initial ink charging step?

No. Follow the setup sequence for your exact model. Initial ink charging prepares the ink delivery system and should not be skipped or improvised.
