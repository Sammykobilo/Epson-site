Epson Nozzle Check and Print Head Cleaning: When to Use Each

December 9, 2023

Learn how an Epson nozzle check helps identify missing print lines and when a print-head cleaning cycle may be appropriate.

Run a nozzle check before cleaning

A nozzle check prints a small pattern that can reveal missing segments, banding, or gaps. It helps you decide whether print-head cleaning is relevant instead of running maintenance by guesswork. Use the printer driver or control panel steps listed for your Epson model.

Use print-head cleaning in moderation

If the nozzle pattern shows gaps, follow the recommended cleaning cycle and print another nozzle check afterward. Cleaning consumes ink, so avoid repeating it many times in a row. If print quality does not improve after the model-recommended steps and rest period, consult the manual or service support.

Rule out other print quality causes

Check paper type, print settings, ink status, and whether the problem appears in every document. A low-resolution source image or incorrect paper setting can look like a printer fault even when the nozzle pattern is normal.

How many times should I run Epson print-head cleaning?

Follow the limit and rest period in the instructions for your printer. Repeated cleaning uses ink and may not resolve a hardware or ink-delivery issue.
