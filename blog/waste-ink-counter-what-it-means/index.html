Epson Waste Ink Pad Counter Warning: What It Means

February 8, 2026

Understand Epson waste ink counter warnings, what a protection counter tracks, and the safe checks to make before considering a reset.

What is the Epson waste ink counter?

An Epson waste ink pad counter is a service-related record associated with ink collected inside the printer during operations such as print-head cleaning. A warning may indicate that the printer has reached a maintenance threshold. It is a prompt to investigate service needs, not proof that every printer has the same physical condition.

Why you should not ignore the warning

The waste ink absorber is a physical component. If it is saturated or leaking, continuing to operate the printer after merely clearing a counter may create ink leakage or other damage. Check the message against the printer manual or qualified service advice and ask for a pad inspection where needed.

What to check before an Epson counter reset

Confirm the exact model, the full message, and the history of maintenance. If the service procedure requires a counter initialization, make sure the relevant physical maintenance has been completed first and that the chosen adjustment utility explicitly supports your model.

Can I reset the Epson waste ink pad counter without replacing the pad?

Do not assume that is safe. First confirm the pad condition and the model-specific service procedure. A counter reset does not perform physical pad service.
