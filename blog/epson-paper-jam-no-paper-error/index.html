Epson Printer Says Paper Jam but There Is No Paper

May 26, 2024

Troubleshoot an Epson paper jam or “no paper” error by checking the paper path, feed rollers, loading, and model-specific instructions.

Inspect the Epson paper path safely

Turn the printer off before checking accessible areas, and follow the manual for opening covers. Remove visible paper slowly in the direction of the feed path. Look for small torn scraps, labels, or foreign objects that can keep a paper sensor from returning to its normal position.

Check paper loading and size settings

Fan and align the stack, avoid exceeding the tray limit, and adjust the paper guides so they touch the stack without bending it. Confirm that the paper size selected in the print dialog matches the paper loaded. Damp, curled, or very thin paper can feed inconsistently.

If the error returns

Do not force a tool or your fingers into inaccessible rollers. A worn roller, obstructed sensor, or damaged mechanism may need professional attention. Record the exact Epson model and error pattern before requesting service.

Can a paper sensor cause an Epson jam error with no paper visible?

Yes. A small paper fragment or a sensor/feed issue can trigger a jam message even when the main paper path looks clear. Follow model-specific instructions and seek service if it persists.
