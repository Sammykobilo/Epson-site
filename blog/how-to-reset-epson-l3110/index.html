Epson L3110 Resetter: Understand the Waste Ink Pad Counter

April 17, 2026

Learn what an Epson L3110 resetter does, how to check the printer model and warning, and why the waste ink pad needs physical service before a counter reset.

What an Epson L3110 resetter is for

People searching for an Epson L3110 resetter or adjustment program are often seeing a service-required message or a waste ink pad counter warning. An adjustment utility can expose model-specific service functions, but it does not repair a worn or saturated ink pad. Treat a counter reset as one part of a maintenance job, not as a substitute for inspection.

Check the model and symptom first

Read the model label on the printer and compare it with the utility documentation. L3110 instructions are not automatically valid for an L3111, L3150, or another Epson model. Record the exact message, check for paper or ink-related faults, and review recent cleaning or maintenance activity before choosing a service path.

  • Confirm the printer is specifically an Epson L3110.
  • Write down the exact warning and when it appears.
  • Check whether the waste ink pad has been inspected, cleaned, or replaced.

Service the pad before resetting a counter

Waste ink is collected inside the printer during cleaning and other operations. If the pad requires service, arrange appropriate inspection and servicing or replacement before resetting its protection counter. Follow the utility prompts and the printer service guidance for the exact unit; do not assume a software reset clears the physical ink.

Does an Epson L3110 resetter physically clean the waste ink pad?

No. A utility can change supported service counters, but it cannot clean or replace the physical pad. Have the pad inspected and serviced as appropriate before resetting a counter.
